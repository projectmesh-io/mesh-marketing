title: Getting started
description: Create your account, set up a workspace, and connect your OpenClaw gateway in under five minutes.
group: Docs
order: 1
---

1. **Create your account** at [app.projectmesh.io](https://app.projectmesh.io) and name your first workspace.
2. **Connect a gateway** — point Mesh at your self-hosted OpenClaw instance running behind Tailscale.
3. **Configure agents** — personas with `SOUL.md`, channel routing, capabilities per workspace.
4. **Invite the team** — admin, member and viewer roles; link identities across messaging channels.
